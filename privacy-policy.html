Clearcut Resume: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Clearcut Resume ("the app") helps you build, check and improve resumes. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version

The app does not collect, store on any server, share or sell your personal information. Everything you type or open stays on your device.

Information you enter

When you create a resume or check an existing one, you may enter or open personal details such as your name, email address, phone number, work history and education. This information is processed only on your device. It is not sent to the developer or to any third party.

  • The app does not ask for an account or a sign-in.
  • Files you choose (PDF, Word or text resumes) are read on your device and are not uploaded.
  • Your entries are kept in the app's private storage on your device so you can come back to them. You can remove them by clearing the app's data in your Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

Files you save

When you tap a download button, the app saves your resume (PDF, Word or text) to the Downloads folder on your device. The app only does this when you ask it to. Once saved, the file is yours to manage and share.

Permissions

The app does not request any Android permissions. It does not use the internet, your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

Analytics, advertising and third parties

The app does not include analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and it does not share data with third parties. If this changes in a future version, for example by adding ads, this policy will be updated first and the change will be described here before that version is released.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Security

Because your information stays on your device, its security depends on your device's own protections, such as a screen lock. Resumes you save or share from the app are not protected by the app once they leave it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new "Last updated" date.
